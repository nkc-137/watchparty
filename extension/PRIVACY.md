# Watch Party — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-19_

Watch Party is a browser extension that synchronizes video playback and relays
chat between people watching the same title on Netflix or Amazon Prime Video. It
is designed to be private and self-hosted.

## Summary

The extension does **not** collect, store, or sell your personal data. It does
not send any data to the developer or to any third party. It communicates only
with the sync server that **you** or your watch-party host operates.

## What the extension accesses

- **Video player state on Netflix and Prime Video.** On watch pages, the
  extension reads the current playback position and play/pause state, and can
  play, pause, or seek the player, in order to keep all participants in sync.
- **Chat messages and reactions you send.** These are text/emoji you choose to
  send to your room.
- **Your settings.** Your sync-server URL, room code, display name, shared
  secret, and overlay preference are stored locally in your browser using the
  extension storage API.

## What is transmitted, and to where

When you join a room, the following is sent **only to the sync server you
configure**:

- Playback actions and position (play/pause/seek and the current timestamp), so
  other participants stay in sync.
- Chat messages and emoji reactions you send, so other participants can see them.
- Your chosen display name.

No video, audio, account credentials, or browsing history is ever transmitted.
No data is sent to the extension's developer or to any analytics or advertising
service.

## Data storage and retention

- Settings are stored locally in your browser and remain until you change or
  remove them, or uninstall the extension.
- The sync server relays messages between participants in real time and is
  operated by you or your host. Any retention on that server is governed by
  whoever runs it. The reference server keeps only transient in-memory room
  state and no persistent logs of chat content.

## Data sharing

We do not sell or share your data with third parties. Data flows only between
you, the other participants in your room, and the server you chose to connect
to.

## Children's privacy

The extension is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data
from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised.
